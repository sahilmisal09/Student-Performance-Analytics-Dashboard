# 03_Business_Analysis

## Overview
This folder contains the Business Analysis artifacts created during the analysis phase of the **Student Performance Analytics & Decision Support System** project.

The documents in this section focus on understanding the existing academic reporting process, identifying business problems, analyzing root causes, defining business rules, and recommending improvements before designing the solution.

---

## Folder Contents

### 01_Current_Process_Analysis
**Purpose**
Documents the existing ("As-Is") process used by Mega Central High School to manage student assessment data and academic reporting.

**Contents**
- Current assessment workflow
- Manual reporting process
- Existing stakeholders
- Current challenges
- Process observations

**Deliverable**
- Current Process (As-Is) Analysis

---

### 02_Pain_Point_Analysis
**Purpose**
Identifies the major business problems experienced by teachers, administrators, and school management during the current reporting process.

**Contents**
- Pain points
- Business impact
- Operational inefficiencies
- Reporting challenges
- Academic monitoring issues

**Deliverable**
- Pain Point Analysis Report

---

### 03_Root_Cause_Analysis
**Purpose**
Investigates the underlying causes of the identified business problems using Business Analysis techniques.

**Techniques Used**
- 5 Whys Analysis
- Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagram
- Cause-and-Effect Analysis

**Contents**
- Root causes
- Problem categorization
- Recommended corrective actions

**Deliverable**
- Root Cause Analysis Report

---

### 04_SWOT_Analysis
**Purpose**
Evaluates the current academic reporting system by identifying its internal strengths and weaknesses, along with external opportunities and threats.

**Contents**
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

**Business Value**
Provides strategic direction for improving academic monitoring and decision-making.

**Deliverable**
- SWOT Analysis Document

---

### 05_Business_Rules
**Purpose**
Defines the business rules that govern student assessment, performance calculation, risk classification, grading, and dashboard reporting.

**Examples**
- Pass/Fail criteria
- Grade calculation
- Performance band classification
- Risk level classification
- Target score thresholds
- Dashboard KPI calculations

**Deliverable**
- Business Rules Document

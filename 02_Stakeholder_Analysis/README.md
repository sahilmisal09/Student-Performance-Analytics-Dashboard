# Stakeholder Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains all stakeholder analysis deliverables prepared for the **Student Performance Analytics & Decision Support System** project.

Stakeholder Analysis is one of the most important Business Analysis activities. It identifies everyone who is affected by the project, analyzes their level of influence and interest, defines their responsibilities, and establishes an effective communication strategy throughout the project lifecycle.

The documents in this folder demonstrate how stakeholders were identified, categorized, prioritized, and engaged to ensure successful project delivery.

---

# Folder Contents

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **01_Stakeholder_Analysis.pdf** | Identifies all key project stakeholders, their roles, expectations, influence, and communication needs. |
| **02_Stakeholder_Register.pdf** | Central repository containing detailed stakeholder information, responsibilities, priorities, and engagement strategy. |
| **03_Power_Interest_Matrix.pdf** | Classifies stakeholders based on their level of influence and interest to determine appropriate engagement strategies. |
| **04_RACI_Matrix.pdf** | Defines project responsibilities by identifying who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for major project activities. |
| **05_Stakeholder_Interview_Questions.pdf** | Structured requirement elicitation questionnaire used during stakeholder interviews to gather business and functional requirements. |

# Stakeholder Prioritization

Stakeholders were categorized using a **Power–Interest Matrix**.

### High Power – High Interest
- Principal
- School Management
- Academic Coordinator

**Engagement Strategy**
- Manage Closely
- Frequent communication
- Requirement validation
- Dashboard reviews

---

### High Power – Low Interest

- IT Administrator

**Engagement Strategy**

- Keep Satisfied
- Technical discussions
- Infrastructure support

---

### Low Power – High Interest

- Teachers
- Students
- Parents

**Engagement Strategy**

- Keep Informed
- Requirement gathering
- User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- Feedback collection

---

### Low Power – Low Interest

- External Parties

**Engagement Strategy**

- Monitor with minimal communication
